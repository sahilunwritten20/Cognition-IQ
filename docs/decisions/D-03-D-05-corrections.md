# D-03 Answer-key change after an exam, D-05 Mark edits after publication (PROPOSED, implemented)

- **D-03 (subject lead / exam committee, needed before grading)**: a wrong key is fixed ONLY through the audited regrade (Runbook B):
  a numbered correction on the quiz with a reason; every affected attempt gets a new grading revision; the original and corrected grades are both kept.
- **D-05 (exam controller, needed before result publication)**: once results are published, a correction additionally requires `approvedBy`
  (the approving person), and students then see the corrected result. There is no other way to change a mark.
- **Options not taken**: editing the live question key (silent, no history), manual per-student mark overrides (not in R1).
- **Status**: defaults are implemented; neither is signed. The audit trail records reason, approver, and counts, never the key or student data.
- **Review trigger**: the college wants a two-person approval inside the system, or per-student manual adjustments.
